# User journeys

## J1: Build history
Sign in, tap Upload, pick a prescription image or PDF, wait for extraction, see editable fields (condition, medicines, dose, date, prescriber and the doctor's phone, registration number, specialty and availability), fix errors, set the dose schedule (slots, before/after food, doses per day, course length), Confirm, land on the timeline with the new entry.

## J2: Recall what worked, or look up a medicine
Open Chat, type "I have a sore throat". Assistant lists medicines used for sore throat before, with dose, prescriber, date and the feedback already recorded. If symptoms are red-flag, assistant says to see a doctor instead. Or type a medicine name: the assistant shows my own records of it and, when saved, a short general description marked as not being advice. Anything else is declined.

## J3: Maintain safety data
On my profile I add allergies; these immediately exclude matching medicines from chat recall. On a prescription I mark each medicine as worked, did not work or side effect.

## J4: Take my doses
Open Today, see doses due by slot. At each confirmed slot a reminder fires; if the dose is not marked, a follow-up fires. Tap taken or skipped. When the course length ends, reminders stop. The user can edit or stop a course at any time.

## J5: Notes and my own reminders
Open Notes and write free text, such as questions for the next visit. From Today, add my own reminder for a medicine I take myself; it appears in Today as "your reminder".

## J7: My profile
Open My profile from the account menu. Fill in name, date of birth, sex, height, weight, blood group, emergency contact and long-term conditions. Save. Call the emergency contact from there.

## J6: Find my doctor
Open a prescription and switch to its Doctor tab, or open Doctors from the timeline. See the doctor's name, specialty, phone, registration number and availability as printed on the prescription. Tap Call, or Share to send the doctor's contact (never my medicines) to someone. From a doctor card, open every prescription that doctor gave me.

## J0: First open and first run
The app opens on a splash with the logo. A visitor then sees four intro pages (what the app does, and last, what it never does), on every start until they sign in (S12), and then the Today tab without being asked to sign in. A visitor can open every tab and read what it does. Sign-in is asked for only when they start something that needs to know who they are: upload, a reminder, a note, the doctors list, or a chat question (including a medicine lookup). They can sign in or create an account; after signing in the thing they asked for continues (S11).

After a new account: short explanation of what the app is and is not (we recall and remind; a doctor diagnoses and prescribes). The user taps to acknowledge once before using the app.
